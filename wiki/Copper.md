# Copper — the wiring

> Nothing in the AI buildout moves electrons without it.

## Role in the chain
Copper is the conductor: it wires the datacenters that house [[Nvidia]]'s GPUs for [[Microsoft]] and others, and it carries the [[Power]] grid that feeds them. But new supply is brutally slow, which makes it a quiet bottleneck behind the whole boom.

## Key facts
- **Thirsty** — a single AI datacenter can use thousands of tonnes of copper.
- **Slow** — a new copper mine takes 10–15 years from discovery to production.
- **The shortfall** — electrifying the world could demand more copper in the coming decades than humanity has mined in all of history.
