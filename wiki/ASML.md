# ASML — the gatekeeper

> The whole leading edge depends on a machine only one company can build.

## Role in the chain
ASML makes the EUV lithography machines that [[TSMC]] (and every advanced fab) needs to print the smallest chips. That makes it a single point of leverage for the entire chain — and a **geopolitical lever**, since export rules decide who is allowed to buy its machines (echoing the chokepoint dynamic at [[RareEarth]]).

## Key facts
- **Sole maker** — ASML is the only company on earth that makes EUV lithography machines.
- **The machine** — one EUV machine costs ~$200M and weighs around 180 tonnes.
- **Gatekept** — export rules now block ASML from selling its most advanced machines to China; lithography is a geopolitical lever.
