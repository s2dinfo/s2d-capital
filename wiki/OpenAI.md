# OpenAI — the demand

> The appetite that pulls on the entire chain.

## Role in the chain
OpenAI is the demand engine: it buys [[Nvidia]]'s GPUs by the tens of thousands, is bankrolled by [[Microsoft]], and is ultimately limited by [[Power]]. Each new model needs far more compute than the last, so its hunger ripples back through [[TSMC]], [[ASML]], [[Copper]] and the grid.

## Key facts
- **Hungrier** — each new AI model tends to need far more compute than the last — often 10×+.
- **Stargate** — OpenAI's announced datacenter buildout was reported in the hundreds of billions.
- **Inference** — answering everyone's prompts is becoming a bigger total compute cost than training the model in the first place.
