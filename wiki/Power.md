# Power — the grid

> The scarce resource in the AI era isn't chips. It's electricity.

## Role in the chain
Power feeds the datacenters that run [[Nvidia]]'s GPUs for [[OpenAI]] and [[Microsoft]]. The grid itself needs [[Copper]] to carry it, and AI's hunger has pulled the old fuel — [[Oil]] and gas — back into the mix. Getting enough power to a site, fast enough, is now the binding constraint on the whole buildout.

## Key facts
- **A small city** — one large AI datacenter can draw as much electricity as a small city.
- **Rising fast** — data centers could reach ~12% of US electricity by 2030, up from 3–4% today.
- **The real bottleneck** — the scarce resource isn't chips, it's power: connecting a big new site to the grid can take years.
