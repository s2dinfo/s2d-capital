# TSMC — the fab

> Almost every advanced chip on earth is made by one company, on one island.

## Role in the chain
TSMC turns designs into silicon. It builds the chips [[Nvidia]] designs (and Apple's, AMD's), using the EUV machines only [[ASML]] makes. Because so much advanced fabrication sits in Taiwan, TSMC is the **geographic chokepoint** of the whole chain.

## Key facts
- **One island** — ~90% of the world's most advanced chips are fabricated in Taiwan.
- **The cost** — a single leading-edge fab costs $20B+ and takes years to build.
- **The chokepoint** — [[Nvidia]], Apple and AMD compete on the shelf, but their chips are made under one roof, by TSMC.
