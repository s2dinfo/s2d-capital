# Microsoft — the capital

> The money and the datacenters behind the demand.

## Role in the chain
Microsoft funds [[OpenAI]] and runs the Azure cloud that buys [[Nvidia]]'s GPUs and rents out the compute — all of it bounded by [[Power]]. Much of its OpenAI investment circles back to itself as Azure spend: a capital loop that quietly underwrites the whole boom.

## Key facts
- **The capex** — Big Tech AI capital spending now runs into tens of billions of dollars per quarter.
- **The backer** — Microsoft is the largest financial backer of [[OpenAI]].
- **The loop** — much of that investment circles back: a large share is spent renting Microsoft's own Azure compute.
