# Oil — the old power

> The energy world AI was supposed to leave behind — and instead leaned on.

## Role in the chain
Oil and gas are the incumbent energy base. AI's electricity hunger revived natural gas as a fuel for [[Power]], and some new datacenters even burn gas on-site when the grid can't connect them fast enough. Oil is also materials — the plastics and lubricants inside the machines, not just the fuel.

## Key facts
- **Still rising** — global oil demand keeps hitting record highs even amid the energy transition.
- **Gas revival** — AI's power hunger has revived natural gas as a source of electricity for [[Power]].
- **On-site** — some new datacenters burn gas in on-site turbines because the grid can't connect them fast enough.

**Amin Nasser** — CEO, Saudi Aramco. *(In the game: the Oil district — refinery, desert sand.)*
