# Nvidia — the GPU designer

> Designs the chips that train and run AI — and builds none of them.

## Role in the chain
Nvidia is the **compute magnet**: it designs the GPUs everyone wants, but the silicon is fabricated by [[TSMC]], whose fabs run on [[ASML]] machines; the boards and datacenters need [[Copper]] and are capped by [[Power]]. The demand pulling on all of it comes from [[OpenAI]] and [[Microsoft]].

## Key facts
- **Zero factories** — Nvidia designs the GPU but owns no fabs; [[TSMC]] builds every single one.
- **Scale** — one frontier AI model can be trained on tens of thousands of Nvidia GPUs at once.
- **The moat** — the real lock-in is software: CUDA makes the chips hard to swap even when rivals match the hardware.
