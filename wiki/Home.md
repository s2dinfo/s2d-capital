# The Chip World

The world that makes the AI era possible — traced from a **wafer to a wire**. Nine nodes, one chain. This is the knowledge graph behind the game; open it in Obsidian and the supply chain draws itself.

## The spine (follow the dependency)
**Demand** — [[OpenAI]] & [[Microsoft]] pull on
→ **compute** — [[Nvidia]] designs the GPUs, which are
→ **fabricated** by [[TSMC]], whose fabs are
→ **enabled** by [[ASML]]'s lithography, all of it
→ **wired** with [[Copper]] and
→ **powered** by [[Power]] (which revived [[Oil]]),
→ every motor and turbine turning on magnets from [[RareEarth]].

## The three tensions (the game's meters)
Every choice in the world trades **Output** vs **Resilience** vs **Sustainability** — you can't max all three. Each node page notes where it pulls.
