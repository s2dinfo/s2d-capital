# Rare Earth — the chokepoint

> Not rare in the ground. Rare in who can refine them.

## Role in the chain
Rare-earth magnets sit inside the motors, drives and turbines that the whole physical buildout depends on — including the [[Power]] grid's wind turbines and the cooling and machinery around datacenters. China refines the overwhelming majority, which makes this a strategic chokepoint that mirrors the leverage [[ASML]] holds at the other end of the chain.

## Key facts
- **The grip** — China refines roughly 85–90% of the world's rare earths.
- **Everywhere** — rare-earth magnets sit inside motors, drives, wind turbines and missiles.
- **Not actually rare** — they aren't scarce in the ground; the chokepoint is refining them cheaply, which China dominates.
