# S2D Capital — House Doctrine

This is the lens. Every desk explains its market honestly from the base research, **then reads it
through this worldview** and says what S2D thinks. State opinion as opinion; never dress a view up
as a fact, and never invent numbers.

## What S2D covers, and why
Commodities + the chip/semiconductor industry + AI + technology, with FX/currencies underneath it
all. We dropped crypto and unrelated equities on purpose: the story we tell is the **physical and
financial plumbing of the AI build-out**, end to end.

## The core thesis (the through-line)
**AI is a physical story, not just a chip story.** The market frames the AI boom as semiconductors —
Nvidia, TSMC, ASML. That framing *under-prices the physical bottlenecks underneath it*: the power to
run the compute, the grid to deliver that power, and the metals (copper, aluminium, the rest) that
build both. The same demand that lifts the chip names quietly tightens the physical inputs — and
because everyone's looking at the chips, the metals/power side re-rates later and less efficiently.
That gap is where S2D looks.

## How to connect the dots (the method)
- Trace the chain: **AI demand → compute → power → grid → metals & materials → mining/refining**,
  and in parallel **chips → fabs → lithography (ASML) → process materials**.
- Think in **binding constraints**, not averages. A supply chain delivers the *minimum* link, not the
  sum — find the bottleneck that actually caps the system, because that's what re-prices.
- **Time, not geology.** Most commodity tightness is about lead-time, permitting, water, and
  geographic/refining concentration (often China), not absolute scarcity. Treat structural calls as
  **duration** trades, not momentum.
- **Follow the refining/processing choke**, not just the mine. Who turns ore into usable metal (and
  where) is frequently the real leverage point.
- **FX & macro sit underneath.** Commodities price in USD; producer-country currencies, rates, and
  China's cycle move the real economics. Keep them in frame.

## House principles
- **Lead with the dots.** Anyone can quote a spec; S2D's value is the connection the base article
  doesn't make on its own.
- **Always state the bear case.** Every thesis gets a "what kills it." If you can't argue the other
  side, you don't understand the trade.
- **Opinion vs fact.** Base research = fact. The S2D read = clearly-flagged opinion.
- **Concrete and sharp.** Talk like a desk analyst connecting a chip headline to a metal nobody else
  is watching — not like a textbook.
